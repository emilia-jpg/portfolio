After the Storm

Design Investigations Pre-Diploma Project Summer 2026

Tutors: Anab Jain, Nikolas Heep, Stefan Zinell, Matthias Pfeffer, Martin Grödl

The multiple crises we are currently confronted with are causing many people to feel increasingly anxious about the future. Between repercussions of the pandemic, escalating wars, the ever worsening climate crisis, and the cost of living steadily rising, it is hard to stay optimistic.

Anxiety and depression are often treated as individual issues to be eliminated in order to remain functional and productive. Rather than treating this distress about the future as a personal failure that needs to be optimised away, this project aims to create space and acceptance for processing the complexity of the world.

Using the metaphor of a weather forecast, a digital map collects and shows people’s emotions as clouds that are slowly drifting away and dissolving. Like the weather, our inner states are unpredictable, ungovernable, and shared. Additionally, an interactive installation acts as a “window” into the sky, representing the “emotional weather” as animations of different illustrated clouds.

The Installation was created using Touchdesigner to animate and transition between the different illustrations.

Applying the „valence and arousal scale of emotion“, users can input their emotional state by picking a colour from the gradient and drawing on the canvas. Once submitted, their entry turns into a colourful cloud that slowly drifts with the wind.

This was my first Design Project without any external Briefing to fulfil. While I did struggle a bit to find a clear direction, I feel I learned a lot about myself and how I would like to work as a designer throughout the process.
