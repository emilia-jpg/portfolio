The Dawn of Fusion

Design Investigations Team Project Summer 2025

Tutors: Anab Jain, Nikolaus Heep, Stefan Zinell, Matthias Pfeffer, Martin Grödl

Team Members: Emma Breitenecker, José Maria De La Garza Flores, Philip Emrich, Parnian Farmani, Maria Schweiger

photo credit: Ronja-Elina Kappl

In a speculative future set in 2065, a breakthrough in nuclear fusion changed everything. Energy became limitless — clean, glowing, and abundant. This monument celebrates that moment. A luminous, floating torus — echoing the shape of fusion reactors — hovers above a solid platform: a symbol of hope, power, and human ambition. Its constant glow represents the promise of light without end.

But what is illuminated — and what remains in shadow?

The grounded platform invites us in, reminding us that even the brightest innovations rest on complex foundations. Fusion freed us from scarcity, but also introduced new risks: consumption without consequence, power without restraint.

“Dawn of Fusion” captures this duality — the thrill of progress and the danger of forgetting its costs. This monument is not a time capsule, but a question posed to the future.

Will abundance lead to responsibility — or indifference?

This Artwork was developed from the brief „monuments of the future” where the students were invited to speculate on different possible futures and create a monument that could be erected in that specific scenario in the year 2065. In this case, Dawn of Fusion is set in a world where energy flows clean and endlessly as a result of a successful break-through in nuclear fusion technology.

A fusion reactor has the form of a torus so that atoms can swirl around in circles resulting in nuclear fusion. Resembling this, the monument consists of an inflatable torus seemingly hovering above an energetically yellow base structure. Inside, pulsing LED lights simulate the flow of particles in fusion reactors, inviting visitors to reflect on our future of energy and the implied possibilities and dangers. We want to provoke questions: Is this a world we want? What can we do to achieve this? In a world where clean energy flows abundantly like water, what remains scarce?

I particularly enjoyed the team work with my colleagues with this project. Throughout the whole process, we were all present and working together every step of the way, learning from each other's strengths.
