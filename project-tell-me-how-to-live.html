Tell me how to live

Design Investigations Team Project Winter 2023

Tutors: Anab Jain, Nikolaus Heep, Stefan Zinell, Grayson Earle

Team Members: TODO: Oulin Chen, Victoria Thierer, Apolonia Bokszycka

“Tell me how to live” is a project exploring the potential future of using personalised AI decision-making to streamline and enhance our daily lives.

In a world overflowing with options, even the most mundane, everyday decisions can be overwhelming. “Tell me how to live” envisions a future where an AI “version of you” assumes the responsibility of making all of these choices on your behalf. Taking algorithmic decision-making to the next level, one would never have to contemplate about what to wear or have for dinner ever again.

The project takes the shape of a physical object representing the connection between humans and their AI proxy. In a short film it additionally examines the themes of trust, dependency and emancipation within this relationship.

This project was created as a student project at Design Investigations. With the briefing „AI Speculation“, students designed AI Agents, speculating about the future relationships between human and Artificial Intelligence.
